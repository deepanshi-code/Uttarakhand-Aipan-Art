The Art Form

What Is Aipan?

A ritual folk art of the Kumaon region, traditionally drawn by women on thresholds, courtyards and altars.

Geru

A red ochre clay base, symbolising the earth and fertility.

Biswar

White lines of ground rice paste, representing purity and peace.

Bindu

Every design begins with a single dot at its centre, from which the pattern grows.

About This Project

Built for Local Artisans

These sites explore how a simple, fast website could help small Aipan art businesses reach buyers beyond their local area, with enquiries going straight to WhatsApp or email.

Both sites work on phones, can be used with a keyboard or screen reader, and meet WCAG AA colour contrast.
