Empowering Lives

Supporting Kumaon's Women Communities

Kumaon Aipan Art was born with a mission: to create direct socio-economic growth for local women in Uttarakhand. By providing painting workshops and direct access to markets, we help them turn ancestral home skills into digital income streams.

Our designs reflect the crisp mountain landscapes and traditional motifs like the eight-petal lotus and Lakshmi footsteps, adapted into minimal aesthetics for modern homes.
