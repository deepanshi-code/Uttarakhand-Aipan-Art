Our Legacy

Folk Art Rooted in the Himalayas

Aipan is a traditional ritualistic folk art native to the Kumaon region of Uttarakhand. Drawn primarily on doors, courtyards, and home entrances, it represents peace, prosperity, and cosmic alignment.

At Devbhoomi Aipan Art, we keep this ancient heritage alive. Each piece is drawn with devotion, beginning with a central dot representing the universe, expanding into intricate mandalas and nature-inspired borders.

"Aipan is not just a drawing; it is a prayer sketched on the earth, welcoming positive energies."
