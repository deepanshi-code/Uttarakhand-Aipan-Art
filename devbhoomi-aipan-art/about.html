The Sacred Legacy of Aipan

Aipan is a traditional folk art native to the Kumaon region of Uttarakhand in the Himalayas. Historically drawn by women at the entrance of houses, courtyards, and places of worship, this art form is deeply intertwined with daily life and special ceremonies.

The base is prepared with Geru, a rich red ochre wet clay plaster, symbolizing the earth and fertility. On this brick-red background, geometric patterns are painted with Biswar—a white paste made from soaked and finely ground rice, representing purity, peace, and spiritual power.

At Devbhoomi Aipan Art, we ensure that this sacred tradition is preserved exactly as it has been for hundreds of years. Every drawing begins with a singular dot (bindu) at the center, signifying the center of creation, from which all other designs expand.
